To get your website live on the internet so people can find it on Google, you need to Host it. Since your project is a single-file website (HTML/CSS/JS), you can do this for free.

Here are the two easiest ways to do it:

Method 1: Netlify (Easiest - Drag & Drop)

This is the fastest way. You don’t need to know any coding to launch it.

  1. Prepare your folder: Put your index.html and your logo.png inside one folder on your computer. Name the folder something like DiagoWebsite.

  2. Go to Netlify: Visit Netlify.com and create a free account.

  3. Upload: Once logged in, go to the "Sites" tab. You will see a box that says "Want to deploy a new site without connecting to Git? Drag and drop your site folder here."

  4. Drag & Drop: Drag your DiagoWebsite folder into that box.

  5. Live: In a few seconds, Netlify will give you a link (like shiny-cool-site.netlify.app). Your website is now live!

  6. Rename: You can go to "Site Settings" and change the name to something like thediagodesigners.netlify.app.

Method 2: GitHub Pages (Professional & Permanent)

This is the industry standard for portfolios.

  1. Create a free account at GitHub.com.

  2. Create a "New Repository" and name it thediagodesigners. Set it to Public.

  3. Click "uploading an existing file" and drag your index.html and logo.png there. Click "Commit changes."

  4. Go to the Settings tab of that repository.

  5. On the left sidebar, click Pages.

  6. Under "Build and deployment", set the Branch to main and click Save.

  7. In about 1-2 minutes, your site will be live at yourusername.github.io/thediagodesigners.

How to make it actually show up on Google Search

Just putting it online doesn't mean it will show up on Page 1 of Google immediately. To "tell" Google your site exists:

  1. Google Search Console: Go to Google Search Console.

  2. Add Property: Enter your new URL (from Netlify or GitHub).

  3. Verification: Follow the steps to verify you own the site (usually by adding a small "Meta Tag" to the <head> section of your code).

  4. Request Indexing: Paste your URL into the top search bar of Search Console and click "Request Indexing." This tells Google's "bots" to come look at your site and add it to search results.

Important Tips for your Project:

  • The Logo: For the logo to show up, the image file must be named exactly logo.png and be in the same folder as the index.html when you upload it.

  • Custom Domain: If you want it to be www.thediagodesigners.com (without the ".netlify" part), you will need to buy a domain name (usually costs about 

            10−10-10−
    15 per year) from sites like Namecheap or Google Domains and connect it to your Netlify account.
